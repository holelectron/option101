## What SpotGamma Offers

SpotGamma is one of the most established names in dealer positioning. It is known for its daily Founder's Notes, key levels and education, and its toolset has grown well beyond daily reports:

* **TRACE**, an intraday S&P 500 heatmap of gamma, delta and charm pressure.
* **HIRO**, a real-time read of options hedging impact.
* **Equity Hub**, covering thousands of stocks and indexes.
* **TAPE**, an options flow tracker, and **COMPASS**, a scanner.
* A **Volatility Dashboard**, and a documented **Vanna Model** for the indexes and ETFs it covers.

So an alternative is less about finding more features and more about a different center of gravity: a map you read strike by strike yourself, a specific framework, flow built into the same workflow, or a live room to learn in.

## How to Choose a SpotGamma Alternative

Before comparing names, decide which of these matters most to you.

* **How you want to read positioning.** Research notes and key levels, or a full strike-by-expiration map you read yourself.
* **Live intraday updates.** Dealer positioning shifts through the session. Check how often each tool refreshes.
* **GEX and VEX.** Gamma for today's levels, vanna for a longer view and for days when volatility spikes. Check whether both are shown by strike and by expiration.
* **Index agreement.** Index traders read SPX, SPY and QQQ together and look for agreement.
* **Ticker coverage.** Index-only, or single stocks too.
* **Flow in the same place.** Whether you can check a print against the positioning at its strike without a second subscription.
* **Learning and support.** Live rooms, lessons and a team you can ask.

💡Core Idea

Exposure maps are models, not exchange-reported numbers. Every platform estimates which side dealers hold and models the exposure that results, so methods differ. Compare how clearly each tool shows its structure, not just whether it has a heatmap.
