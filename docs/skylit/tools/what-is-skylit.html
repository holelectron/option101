What Is Skylit? Is It Legit? Products, Plans and Policies

tools·8 min read
heatseekeroptions-flowtalontools

On this page

Skylit (skylit.ai) is a market-intelligence platform for self-directed traders: one web app that shows where dealers are positioned (Heatseeker), what options traders are doing (Flowseeker), and puts both on a live chart (Atlas), with an AI agent (Talon), paper trading (Nexus), daily Live Stages and the Skylit Academy alongside. It is a legitimate, paid subscription service: market data is licensed directly from OPRA, NASDAQ and CBOE, Skylit is rated 4.9 on both Trustpilot and Whop (as of September 2026), and every plan includes the dedicated Trader Success team. Skylit does not send trade alerts or signals, does not offer refunds, and has three plans (Community, Initiate and Pro), with current prices on the pricing section.

Is Skylit Legit?

These are the facts a careful buyer usually checks, in one place.

  • Licensed data. Market data is licensed directly from OPRA, NASDAQ and CBOE. Dealer positioning is derived by Skylit from that data, not reported by the exchanges.
  • Independent reviews. Skylit is rated 4.9 on Trustpilot and 4.9 on Whop (as of September 2026). Read the reviews there rather than taking our word for it.
  • Real people on every plan. The dedicated Trader Success team helps with the tools in the Skylit Discord, through in-app support and at support@skylit.ai. Pro adds 1:1 Success Sessions.
  • Daily, in the open. Skylit streams daily Live Stages inside the app, where hosts share their screens and talk through the market, the tools and their process.
  • Clear about what it is. Skylit is educational material and analytical tools, not financial advice. It does not send trade alerts or signals; the alerts you set on levels and flow are your own.
  • Plain policies. All sales are final: Skylit doesn't offer refunds, except where the law requires it. There is no trial on paid plans, but you can start free with Live Stages for 60 minutes a day. You can cancel anytime from your billing page, or from your Whop account if you subscribed through Whop; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for and are never charged again. Billing mistakes, like a duplicate charge or a charge after you cancelled, are always corrected: email support@skylit.ai. Subscriptions may auto-renew unless cancelled, as set out in section 15 of the Terms of Service.

Skylit's founder is Glitch, who also founded Heatseeker.

What Skylit Includes

Heatseeker: dealer positioning

Heatseeker shows dealer exposure at every strike (rows) and every expiration (columns) for one ticker. GEX (net gamma exposure) and VEX (net vanna exposure) are on the same board, and you switch between them with the GEX/VEX switch. GEX is used for today's levels; VEX for a longer view and for days when volatility spikes.

The board stars the King Node, the node with the highest absolute GEX, positive or negative. Gatekeepers are prominent nodes between price and the King. Velocity shows which nodes are growing or shrinking, Trinity Mode shows SPXW, SPY and QQQ side by side by default (you can change the instruments), and replay plays past sessions forward, with 1+ years of history at 1-minute granularity. Heatseeker shows where dealers are positioned; it does not predict direction.

Ticker coverage: Heatseeker covers 5,000+ tickers on Pro. Initiate covers 63 index and ETF symbols (SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, VIX, sector and macro ETFs). Community members see index maps through the Heatseeker Bot in the Skylit Discord.

Flowseeker: options flow

Flowseeker streams every options print with where it filled, its premium, its size against open interest, the next morning's change in open interest, and sweep, multi-leg and cross flags. It also has the Dark Feed for off-exchange stock prints, Flow Compass for the market-wide lean, Company Events (earnings, insider filings and Congress disclosures) and Flow Alerts. Flowseeker is included in every plan.

Atlas: the chart

Atlas is a live chart with dealer levels, flow and dark pool on it. It can plot GEX, VEX or GEX+VEX levels on the chart, and it replays sessions. Atlas is included with Pro, and with Initiate for indices and futures.

Talon: the AI agent (Pro)

Talon is the Skylit AI agent. Ask about the chart or map you have open and it answers from live Skylit data: the GEX/VEX map, options flow, dark pool prints, company events and news. It lays out levels, invalidation and risk-to-reward, and it will not tell you what to buy or sell. Talon is included with Pro.

Nexus, Live Stages, Academy and Aegis

  • Nexus (Beta): options paper trading in a $100,000 paper wallet, with trade reviews and a leaderboard. Every plan.
  • Live Stages: daily live rooms inside the app. See live trading rooms for options day traders.
  • Skylit Academy: step-by-step lessons in the app. Every plan.
  • Aegis: the side panel beside your chart, with Live Stages on every plan and a Talon tab on Pro.

Where You Can Use It

Skylit runs in the browser at app.skylit.ai and is fully responsive. You can install it to your phone's home screen as a web app, and Skylit Desktop is in beta for Apple Silicon Macs and Windows 10/11 PCs. Skylit is used for stocks, futures and options: Atlas and Talon are not options-specific, and many members read Heatseeker and Flowseeker levels as context for index futures or shares.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Skylit?

Skylit (skylit.ai) is a market-intelligence web app for self-directed traders. Heatseeker shows dealer positioning (GEX and VEX) at every strike and expiration, Flowseeker streams live options flow, Atlas puts both on a chart, Talon is the Skylit AI agent, and Nexus is paper trading. Every plan also includes daily Live Stages, the Skylit Academy and the Trader Success team.

Is Skylit legit?

Yes. Skylit is a paid subscription service that licenses its market data directly from OPRA, NASDAQ and CBOE, and it is rated 4.9 on Trustpilot and on Whop (as of September 2026). Every plan includes a dedicated Trader Success team in the Skylit Discord, through in-app support and at support@skylit.ai. Skylit is educational material and analytical tools, not financial advice, and it does not send trade alerts or signals.

How many tickers does Skylit cover?

Heatseeker covers 5,000+ tickers on the Pro plan. Initiate covers 63 index and ETF symbols (SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, VIX, sector and macro ETFs), and Community members see index maps through the Heatseeker Bot in the Skylit Discord. Flowseeker is included in every plan.

How much does Skylit cost?

Skylit has three plans: Community, Initiate and Pro, each billed monthly, every 3 or 6 months, or annually. Current prices are on the pricing section at skylit.ai/#pricing, which always shows the live numbers. You can also start free with Live Stages for 60 minutes a day.

Does Skylit offer refunds?

All sales are final. You get full access to Skylit's proprietary, real-time intelligence the moment you join, so we don't offer refunds, except where the law requires it. Not sure yet? Start free with Live Stages, choose a monthly plan, and cancel anytime from your account; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for and are never charged again. Billing mistakes, like a duplicate charge or a charge after you cancelled, are always corrected: email support@skylit.ai.

Does Skylit give trade signals?

No. Skylit gives you the data, the tools and the education to form your own view. Members sometimes share analysis, ideas and education, but Skylit does not send trade alerts or signals. The alerts you set on levels and flow are your own.

Which Skylit products are in Beta or coming soon?

Nexus (paper trading, trade reviews and a leaderboard) is in Beta on every plan. Tempest, the volatility suite, is on Pro. The API and MCP are in Beta. Aethos, futures order execution inside Skylit, is coming in Q4 for Initiate and Pro, and Arc is coming in Q4 for Pro.
