## How to Check Any Trading Tool Before You Pay

The same checklist works for Skylit and for anyone else:

1. **Reviews on independent sites**, not only testimonials on the seller's own page. Look for volume and for recent reviews.
2. **Where the data comes from.** Licensed exchange data (OPRA for options) is a good sign; vague "proprietary sources" with no licensing is not.
3. **Can you watch it being used?** Live sessions, public guides and a free tier let you judge the product before paying.
4. **Red flags:** guaranteed returns, win-rate screenshots with no context, countdown-timer pressure, and signals you are told to copy without understanding.
