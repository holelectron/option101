## How to Choose an Alternative

Before comparing names, decide which of these matters most to you.

* **Every print, in context.** Where each trade filled (bid or ask side), its premium, its size against open interest, sweep, multi-leg and cross flags, and the next morning's change in open interest.
* **Dealer positioning next to the flow.** Whether a print lands on a large GEX node or in an air pocket changes how you read it. Check which exposures a tool shows (GEX, VEX), by strike and by expiration, and how they connect to the feed.
* **Dark pool and company events.** Off-exchange prints, earnings dates, insider filings and Congress disclosures explain a lot of unusual flow.
* **Learning and support.** Live rooms, lessons and a team you can ask matter as much as the data when you are building a process.
* **A place to practice.** Paper trading lets you rehearse a read before you risk money.
* **What each plan actually includes.** Headline tiers differ across the category. Compare what you get on the plan you would buy.

⚠️Warning

Options flow describes what traded, not what happens next. An ask-side call print is not bullish on its own: a hedge, a close or one leg of a spread can all print at the ask. Whatever tool you pick, check structure flags, size against open interest and the next morning's open interest before you read direction.

### FlowAlgo
