## What Skylit Includes

### Heatseeker: dealer positioning

[Heatseeker](https://www.skylit.ai/modules/heatseeker) shows dealer exposure at every strike (rows) and every expiration (columns) for one ticker. **GEX** (net gamma exposure) and **VEX** (net vanna exposure) are on the same board, and you switch between them with the GEX/VEX switch. GEX is used for today's levels; VEX for a longer view and for days when volatility spikes.

The board stars the **King Node**, the node with the highest absolute GEX, positive or negative. **Gatekeepers** are prominent nodes between price and the King. **Velocity** shows which nodes are growing or shrinking, **Trinity Mode** shows SPXW, SPY and QQQ side by side by default (you can change the instruments), and **replay** plays past sessions forward, with 1+ years of history at 1-minute granularity. Heatseeker shows where dealers are positioned; it does not predict direction.

**Ticker coverage:** Heatseeker covers 5,000+ tickers on Pro. Initiate covers 63 index and ETF symbols (SPX, SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA, VIX, sector and macro ETFs). Community members see index maps through the Heatseeker Bot in the Skylit Discord.

### Flowseeker: options flow

[Flowseeker](https://www.skylit.ai/modules/flowseeker) streams every options print with where it filled, its premium, its size against open interest, the next morning's change in open interest, and sweep, multi-leg and cross flags. It also has the Dark Feed for off-exchange stock prints, Flow Compass for the market-wide lean, Company Events (earnings, insider filings and Congress disclosures) and Flow Alerts. Flowseeker is included in every plan.

### Atlas: the chart

[Atlas](https://www.skylit.ai/modules/atlas) is a live chart with dealer levels, flow and dark pool on it. It can plot GEX, VEX or GEX+VEX levels on the chart, and it replays sessions. Atlas is included with Pro, and with Initiate for indices and futures.

### Talon: the AI agent (Pro)

[Talon](https://www.skylit.ai/modules/talon) is the Skylit AI agent. Ask about the chart or map you have open and it answers from live Skylit data: the GEX/VEX map, options flow, dark pool prints, company events and news. It lays out levels, invalidation and risk-to-reward, and it will not tell you what to buy or sell. Talon is included with Pro.

### Nexus, Live Stages, Academy and Aegis

* **[Nexus](https://www.skylit.ai/modules/nexus) (Beta):** options paper trading in a $100,000 paper wallet, with trade reviews and a leaderboard. Every plan.
* **Live Stages:** daily live rooms inside the app. See [live trading rooms for options day traders](https://www.skylit.ai/learn/options-day-trading-live-rooms).
* **Skylit Academy:** step-by-step lessons in the app. Every plan.
* **Aegis:** the side panel beside your chart, with Live Stages on every plan and a Talon tab on Pro.
